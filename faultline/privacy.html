Privacy Policy: FaultLine

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the FaultLine app for iPhone and iPad

English

FaultLine collects no data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no network access. Nothing leaves your device.

Your progress and settings are stored strictly on your device using private app storage. Deleting the app removes all saved progress.

FaultLine reads the iPhone Duo hinge angle or optional device tilt sensors strictly in memory to steer terrain slope in real time. No data is ever collected or shared. The app never accesses your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Contact: github.com/Ebuodinde/iphone-duo-apps/issues or khopilot.app@gmail.com.

Türkçe

FaultLine hiçbir veri toplamaz. Uygulamada hesap, reklam, analiz, izleme veya ağ erişimi yoktur.

FaultLine, vadi eğimini hesaplamak için yalnızca menteşe veya isteğe bağlı eğim sensörünü bellekte okur; hiçbir veri toplanmaz veya aktarılmaz.

Deutsch

FaultLine erfasst keine Daten. Keine Konten, keine Werbung, keine Analyse, kein Tracking, kein Netzwerkzugriff.

FaultLine liest Scharnier- oder Neigungssensoren nur lokal im Speicher aus, um das Talgefälle zu steuern. Es werden keine Daten gesammelt.
