FAQ

How do constructive peaks and Anti-Phase shields work?
Place Tuning Emitters and fold calmly (85°–165°) to align constructive golden Chladni crests (|Ψ| ≥ 1.05) on enemy crystals, or place an Anti-Phase Nullifier (< 0.35) to shield your own.

What are Harmonic Relay Prisms?
Relay Prisms capture passing wave energy and re-radiate it across the central acoustic rift with calibrated phase shifts.

Can I play on a standard iPhone or iPad?
Yes. The full campaign, Daily Cymatic Lock, and Move & Pass 2P Duel work natively with the brass Phase Dial Slider and Device Tilt.
