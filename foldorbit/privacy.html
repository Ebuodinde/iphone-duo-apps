Privacy Policy: FoldOrbit

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the FoldOrbit app for iPhone and iPad

English

FoldOrbit collects no data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no network access. Nothing leaves your device.

Your progress and settings are stored strictly on your device using private app storage. Deleting the app removes all saved progress.

FoldOrbit reads the iPhone Duo hinge angle or optional device tilt sensors strictly in memory in real time. No data is saved externally or transmitted. The app never accesses your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Contact: github.com/Ebuodinde/iphone-duo-apps/issues or khopilot.app@gmail.com.

Türkçe

FoldOrbit hiçbir veri toplamaz. Uygulamada hesap, reklam, analiz, izleme veya ağ erişimi yoktur.

FoldOrbit, yörünge bükümü için yalnızca iPhone Duo menteşe açısını veya isteğe bağlı eğim sensörünü bellekte okur; hiçbir veri toplanmaz veya paylaşılmaz.

Deutsch

FoldOrbit erfasst keine Daten. Keine Konten, keine Werbung, keine Analyse, kein Tracking, kein Netzwerkzugriff.

FoldOrbit liest Scharnierwinkel oder optionale Neigungssensoren ausschließlich lokal im Arbeitsspeicher aus. Es werden keinerlei Daten gespeichert oder gesendet.
