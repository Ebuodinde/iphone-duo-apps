Quick Tips

  • Link a Passage: Select PDF text directly or tap Link Passage on any page to bind a detected sentence to a margin pin.
  • Left-Handed Spread: Open the overflow menu (…) → toggle Left-Handed Spread or configure it in Settings & Privacy.
  • Handwriting Search: Handwritten margin ink is indexed on-device via Vision text recognition automatically after sketching.
  • Standard iPhone & Compact Split View: Switch cleanly between Paper, Margin Notes, and Flashcards using the top pane selector or the bottom Linked Margin Peek strip.
