Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collected

FolioMargin collects no personal data, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or account credentials.

All imported PDFs, handwritten PencilKit drawings, passage highlights, Vision handwriting OCR text indexes, and spaced-repetition flashcard schedules remain stored locally on your device inside the app sandbox.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics libraries, or background network requests.
