FAQ

How does the hinge pedal work?
Stand iPhone Duo partially folded like a small laptop and tilt the top half gently, 10–20° either side of your resting angle. Tap ◎ to calibrate. The hinge is only read between 85° and 165°, so there's never a need to push it flat or snap it shut.

I don't have an iPhone Duo.
On every other iPhone and iPad, tilting the whole device works as the pedal. The touch strip next to the pads always works too. Choose the source in Settings → Pedal.

Where are my loops?
Every session is saved automatically on your device. Open them from the session button (top left) or the ⋯ menu → Sessions.

Why can't I change the tempo?
Tempo locks while loops are recorded so everything stays in time. Clear the tracks or start a new session to change it.

Do my loops keep playing when I close the phone?
Yes. On iPhone Duo the cover screen becomes a tape deck, and playback continues with the screen locked. On other devices, open ⋯ → Tape Deck.

How do I get my music out?
⋯ → Export Audio… renders WAV (24-bit) or M4A and opens the share sheet.
