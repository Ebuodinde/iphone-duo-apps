Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 10-second gentle grace window work?

When a locked session is running closed, gently unfolding opens a 10-second return timer. You can gently close again within 10 seconds to keep your streak, or tap Stay Open to continue cleanly without resetting your session. Rapid hinge folding never adds bonus points or extra time.

Can I use StandBy Clock and Ambient Audio without folding?

Yes. Every focus timer, soundscape mixer slider, lighting warmth control, and Zen sand garden preset can be controlled directly with taps on standard iPhone, iPad, and Split View windows. Standard iPhone also includes an optional Face-Down Desk Ritual in Focus settings.

Why do Live Activities show on the Lock Screen instead of Cover display during backgrounding?

Ensure Live Activities are enabled for SanctuaryTent in iOS Settings. Cover Display and StandBy behavior follow iOS 27.1 system presentation rules.
