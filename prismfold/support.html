FAQ

How does bending light with the hinge work?
Hold iPhone Duo like an open book and fold calmly between 85° and 170°. As the crease folds, the light beam crossing the center deflects into mirrors, prisms and lenses on the right leaf. There is never any need to slam the device shut or force it flat.

What is the Cover Prism Ring (Showcase Level 3 & Chapters IV–V)?
When a white beam hits the translucent seep portal beside the crease, close iPhone Duo (or tap the Prism pill in the top bar) to rotate the ring on the cover screen until it splits white light into Red, Green and Blue channels—then reopen calmly to route all three beams.

I'm playing on a standard iPhone or iPad.
Every single level works seamlessly using the on-screen glass slider, ±1° / ±5° crystal steppers, or optional Device Tilt (Settings → Angle Input).

How do I rotate a mirror, prism or lens?
Tap any rotatable optic (marked with a glowing cyan/gold ring) on the board to select or rotate it in 15° crystal steps, or use the -15° / +15° bar below.
