Privacy Policy

Data Not Collected. CueBridge operates 100% locally on your device. It does not require accounts, contain ads or third-party SDKs, collect analytics or telemetry, or transmit anything over a network.

  • Camera & Microphone: Used exclusively on-device to preview framing, meter audio headroom, and save local H.264 .mp4 takes inside your app container.
  • Speech Recognition: When Voice Follow is enabled, CueBridge configures Apple's speech recognizer with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true so audio is never uploaded to the cloud; if no offline language pack is available on device, timed scroll fallback activates automatically.
  • Photo Library: Accessed in addOnly mode solely when you tap Save to Photos on a recorded take.
