Quick Tips

  • Cover Lens Edge Calibration: Open Studio Settings (gear in the overflow menu) to nudge cover teleprompter text toward the Top, Bottom, Leading, or Trailing camera edge and enable horizontal Beam-Splitter mirroring.
  • Markup Syntax: Add [pause], [pause 2s], [es] for timed breaths, wrap *key phrases* in asterisks, and start headings with § Section or # Section for instant tabletop jumps.
  • Breathing Pre-Roll: Recording opens with a calm 3-second inhale countdown so your voice starts steady; toggle Skip 3s Breathing Countdown in Studio Settings for instant capture.
  • Standard iPhone & iPad: Use the semi-transparent overlay card on standard iPhone (edge-aligned via ArrangementView(.overlay)) or split-pane Director Studio + Scripts & Takes on iPad.
